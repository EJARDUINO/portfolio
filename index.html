Featured Project

Solar-Powered Smart Bell System Featuring Web-Based Automation

An eco-friendly automated school bell that removes manual ringing errors and delays, using a real-time clock for scheduling and a local web interface for remote setup. Category: embedded systems, IoT and renewable energy prototyping.

Key Features

  • Zero-delay synchronization: 0 sec mean delay using a hardware Real-Time Clock.
  • Local web server: ESP8266 interface to adjust bell schedules, modes and durations wirelessly, with no internet needed.
  • Precision timekeeping: DS3231 RTC over the I²C protocol.
  • Status and override: 16×2 LCD readout plus physical push buttons.
  • Off-grid power: solar collector with a rechargeable battery.
  • Serial commands: custom parsing such as SET_TIME:HH:MM, SET_MODE:value, SET_DURATION:value.

Personal Journey

Moving to Mapúa's fast-paced engineering program tested my adaptability. I had gaps in foundational math, so I knew success would depend on my own discipline.

How I Overcame It

Daily problem-solving

3 to 5 hours every day on problem-solving and breaking down concepts.

Early exam prep

Starting 3 to 7 days ahead, so cramming is never the plan.

Real mastery on ALEKS

Working through proofs on my own instead of relying on shortcut generators.

Active study techniques

Active recall, the blurting method for formulas, and Pomodoro sessions of 30 to 45 minutes, with videos from The Organic Chemistry Tutor and Crash Course when I'm stuck.

Focus & wellness

Cutting non-essential gaming and managing stamina with healthier alternatives to caffeine.

Reflection. With structured perseverance I got through Math 165 and 166 and built the confidence for Math 176 and my major CPE subjects. Technical skill is built through consistency, effort and structured problem-solving.
